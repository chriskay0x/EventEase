What you can do

Check your card details

Make sure the card number, expiry and CVC are correct, and that your card has sufficient funds for the total amount.

Try another card or bank

Some banks block online or large transactions by default. A different card often goes through instantly.

Contact your bank

Ask them to authorize online transactions, then retry. Your booking reference is kept safe either way.

Need help? Write to support@eventease.com with your reference number.
